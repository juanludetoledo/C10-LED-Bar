# C10 LED Control · Privacy

Updated: November 2026.

C10 LED Control talks only to the C10 LED controller in your car, over Bluetooth (BLE).
It has no account, no advertising and no analytics, and it sends nothing to any server.

- **Settings and animations** are stored locally on your phone and in the controller's memory.
- **Bluetooth / Nearby devices** permissions are used to find and connect to the controller.
- **Approximate location** is used only for *By sun* mode, to work out sunrise and sunset on the
  phone itself. It is never sent anywhere.
- **Exact alarms** and **start-up** permissions apply the day / night schedule at the right time.

GitHub and Telegram handle visits, downloads and messages according to their own policies.
